# Formal Complaint: Writing a Formal Email

## 1. Purpose

A formal complaint is a written expression of dissatisfaction with a product, service or situation. It normally explains what happened, why it was unacceptable and what action the recipient is expected to take.

- **complaint** (noun) — жалоба, претензия: *I made a formal complaint.*
- **complain** (verb) — жаловаться: *I complained to the company about its service.*
- **compliant** (adjective) — соответствующий требованиям: *The vehicle is compliant with safety regulations.* Do not confuse this word with **complaint**.

Useful patterns: **complain to** someone **about** something; **make / submit / lodge a complaint**; **have grounds for complaint**.

## 2. Formal vs informal English

| Feature | Informal | Formal |
|---|---|---|
| Phrasal verbs | Please look into this. | Please investigate this matter. |
| Contractions | I haven't received a reply. | I have not received a response. |
| Vocabulary | I got an answer. | I received a response. |
| Idioms | The staff were rushed off their feet. | The staff were extremely busy. |
| Passive voice | You cancelled the service. | The service was cancelled. |
| Linking words | But / so / also | However / therefore / furthermore |
| Emotional language | The service was awful! | The service was unsatisfactory. |
| Requests | I want my money back. | I would like to request a refund. |
| Informal abbreviations | ASAP | As soon as possible |
| Greeting | Hi! | Dear Sir or Madam, |
| Sign-off | Cheers | Yours faithfully, |

These are **tendencies, not absolute grammatical rules**. Formal English can use phrasal verbs, active voice, short sentences and simple vocabulary. Clarity is more important than complexity. In a complaint, active voice can make responsibility clearer: *The driver failed to stop.*

### Expressing a general rule using the passive voice

- Phrasal verbs **are not used** in formal writing. — Фразовые глаголы не используются в формальной письменной речи. (Grammatically correct, but too categorical.)
- Phrasal verbs **are used less frequently** in formal writing. — Фразовые глаголы реже используются в формальной письменной речи.
- Informal phrasal verbs **should be avoided** where a clearer alternative exists. — Следует избегать разговорных фразовых глаголов, если есть более ясная альтернатива.

Present Simple passive: **am / is / are + past participle**.

Modal passive: **modal verb + be + past participle**.

## 4. Structure of a formal complaint email

| Section | Purpose | Useful expressions |
|---|---|---|
| Subject | Identify the issue | Formal complaint regarding ... |
| Salutation | Address the recipient | Dear Sir or Madam, |
| Introduction | State the purpose | I am writing to raise a formal complaint regarding ... |
| Background | Provide facts, including date and place | On [date], I was waiting at ... |
| Complaint | Explain what went wrong | Unfortunately, ... / The driver failed to ... |
| Consequences | Explain the impact | As a result, ... / This caused ... |
| Requested action | State what should be done | I request that you investigate this incident. |
| Conclusion | Request a response | I look forward to receiving your written response. |
| Sign-off | Close politely | Yours faithfully, |

### British correspondence conventions

- **Dear Sir or Madam** → **Yours faithfully**
- **Dear Mr Smith / Dear Ms Smith** → **Yours sincerely**
- **Dear Customer Service Team** → **Kind regards** (common in business emails)

## 5. Worked example: a real bus service complaint

This learning example is adapted from a previously drafted complaint concerning Stagecoach in Elgin, dated 10 September 2025. The driver failed to stop at a designated bus stop where the complainant and another passenger were waiting. The complainant was late for work as a result.

The wording is reconstructed for learning purposes; it is not a verbatim copy of the original correspondence. The route number, stop name and scheduled time are not available here and have not been invented.

### Informal version

The bus went past my stop without stopping, even though another passenger and I were waiting there. I ended up being late for work. I want Stagecoach to look into what happened, sort out the problem and get back to me.

### Formal version

**Subject: Formal complaint regarding a bus driver's failure to stop**

Dear Customer Service Team,

I am writing to raise a formal complaint regarding an incident involving a Stagecoach bus in Elgin on 10 September 2025.

The driver failed to stop at a designated bus stop where another passenger and I were waiting. As a result, I was late for work. This caused unnecessary inconvenience and disruption.

I consider this standard of service unacceptable. I request that you investigate the incident and take appropriate corrective action to prevent similar incidents in the future.

I would also appreciate a written response explaining the outcome of your investigation and the measures taken to address the issue.

Kind regards,

[Name]

### Comparison of the two versions

| Informal | Formal | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| The bus went past my stop without stopping. | The driver failed to stop at a designated bus stop. | Precise description of the failure and responsibility. |
| I ended up being late for work. | As a result, I was late for work. | Explicit link between the incident and its consequence. |
| I want you to look into it. | I request that you investigate the incident. | Firm but professional request. |
| Sort out the problem. | Take appropriate corrective action. | Specifies the type of action expected. |
| Get back to me. | Provide a written response. | Specifies the expected response. |

## 6. Preparing a real complaint

Before sending, include the relevant facts that can be verified: the date, time, route, direction, stop, booking or ticket reference (if applicable), what happened and the consequences. State the remedy you actually want, such as an explanation, investigation, corrective action or refund. Do not invent missing facts or request a refund if there is no relevant payment.

If a complaint has been submitted before, useful expressions include:

- **I previously raised this issue with your company.**
- **Despite my previous correspondence, this issue remains unresolved.**
- **I no longer use your services, but I still have grounds for complaint.**

Use these only when they accurately describe the situation.

## 7. Common errors

| Incorrect / unnatural | Correct / natural |
|---|---|
| I need to create a complaint email. | I need to write a formal complaint email. |
| Thank you for your compliant. | Thank you for your complaint. |
| I have what to complain at. | I still have something to complain about. |
| I complained on the bus company. | I complained to the bus company about its service. |
| Phrasal verbs do not used. | Phrasal verbs are not used. |
| I look forward to hear from you. | I look forward to hearing from you. |
| I demand you to investigate. | I request that you investigate. |

## 8. Main principle

A formal complaint should be **clear, factual, specific, polite and firm**. Formality is not about making every sentence longer or more complicated.
